08 Common questions

What homeowners ask before signing.

Does my roof actually have hail damage?

From the ground it's hard to tell. We bring a drone and do a 21-point inspection — including soft metals (gutters, vents) and granule loss in test squares — and send you the report in 24 hours.

Will my insurance cover a new roof?

If we document covered storm damage, almost always — yes. We meet your adjuster on the roof and submit an Xactimate-format scope. Most clients pay only their deductible.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are tear-off and re-roof in a single day. Full exterior projects with siding and gutters typically wrap in 7–11 days, weather permitting.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — 0% APR options for qualified homeowners through Hearth and GreenSky. You can pre-qualify in about 60 seconds without affecting your credit.

What's your warranty?

Lifetime workmanship from Purple Storm plus up to 50-year manufacturer coverage from GAF and Owens Corning, depending on the system you choose.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully — Minnesota Residential Building Contractor license, general liability, and workers' comp on every crew member. We'll send the certificates before we send the contract.
